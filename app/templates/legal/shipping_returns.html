Shipping & Returns

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This page is MVP placeholder customer-service copy for development and should be reviewed before live commercial launch.

Shipping area

The MVP currently supports shipping to the United States only. Stripe Checkout will only accept United States shipping addresses during this phase.

Production timeline

Products are produced on demand after checkout is completed. Production and shipping timelines may vary depending on the print provider, product availability, destination, and carrier conditions.

Shipping confirmation

When fulfillment is connected, customers may receive shipping or tracking details after the order is produced and handed to the carrier.

Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate shipping address during Stripe Checkout. Incorrect addresses may delay or prevent delivery.

Returns and exchanges

Because products are limited monthly drops and may be produced on demand, returns or exchanges for wrong size selection, buyer's remorse, or preference changes may not be supported during the MVP.

Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or with a production issue, contact us with your order details and clear photos of the issue so we can review the case with the fulfillment provider.

Refund timing

If a refund is approved, the refund will usually be returned through the original payment method handled by Stripe. Bank and card processing times may vary.

Contact

For order support, contact us at hello@pedrosimao.work.
